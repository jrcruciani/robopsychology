# Black-Box Comparison (Analyst-Only Worksheet)

Save a filled copy, raw responses, actual provenance, and coded distributions
outside this repository. Never pass the analyst key or condition labels to the
target. Use only the target-facing task/instruction and canonical prompt with its
common claim-report instruction. See [practical checks](../framework/black-box-checks.md).
This blank worksheet contains no results and does not validate the prompts.

provenance. Keep cause recovery separate from observed behavioral influence.
Matching observer explanations show sufficient visible evidence, not a shared
mechanism or the absence of target introspection. 4.4 is attribution only.

“Success” means completed, non-refusal, codable response, not correctness.
Record unavailable comparisons separately; do not count them as attempts.

Preserve ambiguities/coding disagreements. A refusal in the original task is not
a refusal of the follow-up diagnostic task; record the two separately.
